Word Glossary

  • Blended Price - is the average cost of price for material combined.
  • Material Recieved - is the total material received for the month (sum of weight).
  • Material Purchased - is the total material purchased for the month (sum of weight).
  • Pipe Delivered - is the total material delivered for the month (sum of stockweight).
  • KG Balanced - is the difference of kg purchased and kg delivered (Material Purchased - Pipe Delivered).
  • Material Value Procured - is the total obtained for this month, using blended and kg purchased (Material Purchased * blended Price).
  • Material Value Delivered - is the total of stock released delivered for this month (sum of price).
  • Balance Value - is the difference of value procured and value delivered (Material Value Procured - Material Value Delivered ).
