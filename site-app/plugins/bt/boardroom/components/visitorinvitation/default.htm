O.H.S INDUCTION FOR VISITORS

  • Before coming to BT Industrial site, you need to know more about our work activities, hazards, dangers, and EHS rules on the site.
  • Ensure that you sign the register for visitors at the security gate accordingly.
  • You are responsible for your own safety and those around you.
  • Ensure that you wear the correct PPE when entering the workshop area. (Safety shoes, hardhat and reflector)
  • You are only allowed to move around with BT employees or representatives.
  • Follow instructions from this person and be alert of the high-risk activities around you.
  • There are numerous safety signs. Take note of these signs and obey the signs.
  • This site has areas where material and or equipment could fall onto you. Be aware of what is above you. Do not walk under any suspended loads.
  • Be careful when walking on site as you can fall over equipment, material or into a hole - follow the designated walkways and you should be free of any tripping hazards.
  • Machinery has the right of way and the operator’s vision is limited. Take care and stay out of their way.
  • Do not enter any area where white and red tape or physical barricading has been erected around an activity.
  • Do not climb onto any high structures including scaffolds.
  • Do not enter the site when under the influence of alcohol or any other narcotics.
  • Do not get involved in any physical assault while on site.
  • Report any injury or incident to the Site Management before you leave site.
  • You are required to be safety conscious, e.g standing still when you answer your Cell phone.
  • Make use of all your senses & do anything possible to prevent accidents.
  • Do not interfere with any work activities.
  • You are not allowed to operate or be inside any Machinery / Plant unless it’s in your scope of authority and you have your operating certificates with you. The site management will then authorise the work.
  • Report back to the security gate before departure.
